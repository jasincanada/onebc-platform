Short forms.

On the other pages, a short form has a dotted underline. On a computer, rest on it and the meaning appears. On a phone, use this page.

Short formMeaning
a.m.Before noon.
B.C.British Columbia. Also written BC.
CRTCThe Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission. The federal body that sets the rules for phone calls in Canada.
GSTThe federal goods and services tax. It is not included in the fee, and it is not added to it.
NodeShort for Node.js. The program that runs the desk on the server.
OneBCThe party this briefing is written for. The name is OneBC.
p.m.After noon.
PostgresShort for PostgreSQL. The database that holds the list, the notes, and the removal list.
PSTThe British Columbia sales tax. It is 7% when a sale is taxable. It is not included in the fee.
VoIPA phone call carried over the internet. On these pages it appears in the name VoIP.ms, the Canadian phone company.

These are the only short forms on the site. A company name that is not a short form, such as Telnyx or Cloudflare, is not listed here.
