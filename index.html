Pilot desk

This is a pilot demonstration: one working desk, not a finished product and not a full campaign tool.

Open pilot.cryptocomputer.ca. The live version is 0.2.9. A setup walk-through takes you through a simple page. You do not look at the server. The footer there links to what changed.

A live try allows only 5 phone numbers, 5 text numbers, and 5 email addresses. A real call, text, or email is allowed only inside those limits. A wider live campaign stays off.

The phone companies are VoIP.ms, Twilio, Telnyx, Plivo, and Bandwidth. You bring your own account. None of them is connected on the live desk yet, so a client cannot place a live call today.

Mail can be sent through Google, Microsoft, or your own mail server. Signing in is not permission to send mail. Mail send is not connected.

Sign in with Microsoft or Google, plus a code or a passkey. People can be in groups, and the highest group wins. Custom roles have a permissions screen. A large red button asks twice before a person deletes their own account or leaves a role.

The price is $4,000 US dollars before tax, for time and machines only. British Columbia sales tax is not added on this custom desk.
